Optimizing Telemarketing for Term Deposit Subscriptions

Analyzed a bank's telemarketing campaign dataset to predict which customers are most likely to subscribe to a term deposit. Combined EDA, Logistic Regression, and a live Streamlit app — enabling sales teams to prioritize high-conversion leads and reduce wasted call volume.

Key Insight: Previous campaign outcome (poutcome) is the strongest conversion predictor — customers with a positive history have a significantly higher subscription probability.
